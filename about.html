Our history

A practice built on experience and access

Mahlangu & Associates Inc. was established in November 2016 under the leadership of Nhlanhla Goodwill Mahlangu as sole director. The practice was created to provide legal services suited to the individual requirements of clients while drawing on experience in litigation, employment law, government law, investigations, contracts and legal advisory work.

The office at No. 6 Geranium Drive, Riviera Park, Mahikeng is positioned near the North West High Court, provincial government departments, the North West Provincial Legislature, North-West University and municipal institutions. The firm can also attend to matters outside Mahikeng where required.

The client base developed across individuals, emerging enterprises, private companies, municipalities, provincial departments, the provincial legislature and state-owned entities. A network of associates extends professional capacity while maintaining direct client attention.

The director

Nhlanhla Goodwill Mahlangu

The director holds an LLB degree from the University of North West, now North-West University, and a Certificate in Legislative Drafting from the University of Johannesburg. The director was admitted as an attorney of the High Court of South Africa in November 2005 and holds a right of appearance in the High Court.

Following articles of clerkship with Kgomo Mokhetle and Tlou Attorneys, the director joined the North West Provincial Government’s Office of the Premier Misconduct Unit, gaining experience as a misconduct investigator, prosecutor and presiding officer.

Later responsibilities as Senior State Law Advisor included provincial litigation management, contracts, legal opinions, commissions of inquiry and legislative drafting. The director also served as Manager: Legal Services for the North West Department of Sport, Arts and Culture.

After nine years in provincial government, ending in July 2015, the director joined a Schedule 3C state entity as legal advisor and company secretary. This career background provides detailed knowledge of public administration, institutional processes and government as a major employer and client.

The firm identifies as B-BBEE compliant and 100% black owned, with a commitment to diversity, equal opportunity and transformation.
